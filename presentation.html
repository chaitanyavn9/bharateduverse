Business Model

Revenue From Colleges, Never From Students

Students and parents use BharatEduVerse free — always. Colleges pay to reach the right students at the right time.

Phase 1 ✅
Free Discovery Platform
Build traffic and trust. Students discover career paths at no cost. Data is the asset.
Phase 2
College Enhanced Profiles
Colleges pay for rich listings — placements data, photos, scholarship info, testimonials. Free basic listing always available.
Phase 3
Analytics Dashboard
Colleges get data: which students viewed their profile, which careers they're interested in, which exams they're preparing for.
Phase 4
Opt-in Lead Generation
Students who want to be contacted by colleges opt in. Colleges pay per verified, relevant lead.
🚫
Never — Paid Rankings. Trust is the product. We will never compromise it.
